# Global News Scan

Date the report in Singapore time and state the research cutoff. Open with a brief executive snapshot. Aim for 10–15 substantive, distinct items, with more when warranted. Cover multiple developments in the same country when material. If the first pass yields fewer than 10, broaden the regions, topics and primary sources checked. If still short, explain the shortfall. Use dated follow-ups or watchpoints only when they add useful information.

## Standing coverage

Check all five sections and their topics every run. Keep the headings even when a subsection has no material verified change. Give a short status or the next concrete event in that case. Prioritize Singapore, Malaysia and China, followed by relevant Southeast Asian, African and Middle Eastern developments. Scan consequential events worldwide.

1. **World News.** Check the Iran war and Middle East spillovers, US politics and economy, China politics and economy, and Southeast Asia with dedicated attention to Singapore business and Malaysia. Check other conflicts, sanctions, trade restrictions, disruptions to energy or shipping, and major economic or policy shocks. Explain the channel to markets or Global South Advisory (GSA), such as oil or LNG, shipping and insurance, rates, FX, sanctions, supply chains or investment flows. Distinguish verified developments, claims by interested parties and scenarios. Check maritime advisories or observed vessel movements when shipping disruption is central.
2. **Technology.** Cover sodium-ion and other battery energy storage, grid projects, procurement, manufacturing and adoption. Distinguish pilot, procurement, preferred bidder, award, commissioning and operation. Separate sodium-ion from other sodium-based chemistries and project-wide pricing from battery-only pricing. Cover AI models, APIs, agents, enterprise deployments and infrastructure. Check DeepSeek's [API changelog](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/updates/), [GitHub releases](https://github.com/deepseek-ai) and [model repository](https://huggingface.co/deepseek-ai) for material changes. Check Chinese and global developers.
3. **Stock Markets.** Cover STI, DBS, OCBC, UOB, US benchmarks and AI infrastructure names, China/Hong Kong and relevant Southeast Asian markets. Verify local closing prices and label each session date. On holidays and weekends use the most recent completed session for each market. Compute daily moves from the preceding completed close and label weekly moves against the previous week's final close. State the basis if using adjusted prices. For oil identify the futures contract and use the same contract for the weekly comparison. Cover earnings, rates, FX and other catalysts when material. For quantitative implications, align signals to when they were observable across time zones and distinguish earnings effects from market-wide moves.
4. **Trending Applications.** Give practical app or agent releases and meaningful adoption news a dedicated product section. Search in English and Chinese for DeepSeek, Alibaba/Qwen, ByteDance/Doubao, Moonshot/Kimi, Tencent, Baidu and emerging teams, plus global providers. Identify what the product does, release stage, access tier, mainland versus international or Singapore availability, verified pricing and one concrete use case. Identify unverified access or pricing as such. Avoid duplicating model or infrastructure stories unless there is a distinct product change. Label prereleases, limited pilots and provider-reported adoption figures.
5. **GSA Opportunities.** Surface actionable tenders, projects, partnerships and policy openings, including infrastructure, power and special economic zones. Verify issuer, reference, scope, procurement stage, eligibility and deadline where available. Link directly to the issuer. Separate active opportunities from early leads and awards. Explain the practical qualification step or route to participation.

## Mandatory Singapore procurement check

- Open the official [GeBIZ portal](https://www.gebiz.gov.sg/) every run. Search public listings and verify individual notices for newly published and still-open tenders, quotations and RFIs in battery storage, solar, infrastructure, transport and AI or digitalisation. If the dynamic listing does not load, try official indexed notice pages and describe the scope achieved.
- Recheck prior material leads by reference, including JTC mobile solar-plus-storage (JTC000ETT26000106) while actionable. Check visible corrigenda, revised closing times and compulsory briefing or registration dates. Treat sign-in-only amendments as unverified.
- Report agency, reference, scope, status, closing date and time in SGT and a direct official notice link. Include any public eligibility criteria, mandatory site briefing or registration deadline. Flag restricted documents. State whether the check found relevant results, found none, or was unavailable or incomplete.

## Verification and delivery

- Use fresh web research. Prioritize official issuers, exchanges, regulators, central banks, port and shipping authorities, company filings and multilaterals. Cross-check consequential or disputed claims independently where available. Verify event and publication dates against the edition cutoff, open original sources and distinguish primary evidence from reporting or commentary.
- Use standard web research for verification. Use optional [DeepSeek Search](references/deepseek-search.md) for discovery when configured, especially for Chinese-language and undercovered topics. If it is unavailable, continue with standard web research. Always complete the DeepSeek product and release checks independently.
- Give each item a headline, dated summary, practical business or investment implication and evidence links. Use tables for market closes and comparable opportunities. Include useful source visuals or links to original maps and charts when available.
- Use prior editions and feedback when available. Compare by event, project or tender reference and surface material changes only. Retain useful open threads and proven sources, drop persistently low-signal ones and test at least one credible new source, geography or search angle. If history is unavailable, start fresh and avoid claiming continuity.
- End with a compact **Sources scanned** list of clickable sources actually checked, including useful sources that did not yield a headline. Add a short **Watch next** only for useful open threads. Name the source, next event or measurable trigger so a future run can pick it up. Keep the reusable workflow free of private client data and credentials.
